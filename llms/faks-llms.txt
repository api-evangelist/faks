# Faks

> Faks is a French SaaS platform that digitizes the commercial relationships across the pharmacy ecosystem — connecting pharmacies, pharmacy groups (groupements), and laboratories to manage suppliers, commercial claims and disputes (litiges), expired/returnable products, promotions, and sell-in/sell-out performance. Backed by Seedcamp. No public developer API is published at this time.
